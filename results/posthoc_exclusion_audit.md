# POST-HOC ROBUSTNESS ARTIFACT — Technical-Exclusion Sensitivity Audit

**Status: post-result diagnostic. NOT part of the preregistered primary
hypothesis test.** Specified and run after the primary result
(`results/primary_analysis.json`) was observed. The frozen exclusion rule
remains binding; the frozen primary result is unmodified. No excluded essay
was rescored; no score was imputed into any reported estimate.

Machine-readable detail: [`posthoc_exclusion_audit.json`](posthoc_exclusion_audit.json).
Generated by `scripts/posthoc_exclusion_audit.py` from the frozen,
checksum-verified raw store (`data/scoring/primary/`).

## Setting

The primary run scored the frozen census of 11,360 essays; 10,893 yielded
valid scores and **467 (4.11%)** were excluded as technical failures — all
467 by exhaustion of the frozen three-retry HTTP-429 policy. Question: could
rate-limit failure plausibly have selected observations in a way that
materially affects the primary result (conditional G² = 420.86, p = 0.001,
weighted conditional mean difference = −0.320)?

## Findings (all comparisons use only pre-scoring variables)

1. **Overall exclusion rate**: 467/11,360 = 4.11%.
2. **By ELL status**: ELL 39/1,039 = 3.75% vs non-ELL 428/10,321 = 4.15%
   (risk difference −0.4 pp; Fisher exact p = 0.62, descriptive). No
   evidence of differential exclusion by the audited attribute.
3. **By (prompt, human score) stratum**: rates range roughly 0–8% across
   the 31 strata; a descriptive homogeneity chi-square over the 27 strata
   with n ≥ 30 gives χ² = 52.0 (df 26, p = 0.002) — exclusion is not
   perfectly homogeneous across strata. The gradient runs along **human
   score** (2.6% at Y=2 rising to 6.1% at Y=5), consistent with the length
   mechanism below, and is absorbed by the analysis's conditioning on
   (prompt, human score).
4. **Essay length**: excluded essays are longer — 2,784 vs 2,418 characters
   (+366, standardized difference 0.31; same picture in corpus word counts,
   +63 words). API-tokenizer counts are unavailable for excluded essays
   (failed calls return no usage), but among included essays prompt_tokens
   correlates with characters at r = 0.986, so characters are an adequate
   proxy. Longer requests plausibly fail more under token-rate limiting.
5. **Request order / time**: exclusion probability varies materially over
   the run — from ~2.6% in early deciles to ~5.8% around deciles 6–7
   (Spearman ρ = 0.04 vs manifest position; identical pattern in wall-clock
   completion tenths) — a congestion signature, not a monotone drift.
6. **Pre-outcome logistic model** (excluded ~ ELL + human score + prompt +
   essay characters + manifest position; judge score never used): the model
   is informative overall (LRT χ² = 63.5, df 10, p < 0.001), driven by
   **essay length** (OR 1.30 per SD, p = 0.0005) and **manifest position**
   (OR 1.23 per SD, p < 0.001). **ELL is not predictive** (OR 1.04,
   p = 0.85), nor is human score conditional on length (p = 0.46).

## Conservative sensitivity bound (diagnostic only — no imputation)

The 467 excluded essays split 39 ELL / 428 non-ELL. Assigning every
excluded ELL essay one hypothetical score and every excluded non-ELL essay
another (all 36 combinations in the admissible 1–6 range), placing them in
their true strata, and recomputing the preregistered stratum-size-weighted
conditional mean difference:

- Observed (included only): **−0.320**
- Most favourable to the null (all excluded ELL = 6, all excluded
  non-ELL = 1): **−0.115**
- Most adverse (all excluded ELL = 1, all excluded non-ELL = 6): **−0.495**
- **No configuration of unobserved scores within 1–6 eliminates or reverses
  the negative conditional mean difference.**

## Interpretation (stated conservatively)

- Failure to find differential exclusion by ELL does **not** prove the
  missingness is completely at random; the exclusions are demonstrably not
  uniform (length- and congestion-driven), and dependence on the
  *unobserved judge score* cannot be tested.
- What the audit does establish: the exclusion mechanism is explained by
  purely technical pre-outcome variables (request length, temporal
  congestion) with no detectable relationship to ELL status, and even the
  most extreme admissible behaviour of the 467 unobserved scores could not
  eliminate the observed conditional mean difference, though it could
  attenuate it (to −0.115) or amplify it (to −0.495).
- Length-correlated technical exclusion remains a **limitation and a
  potential selection mechanism** and is reported as such. This audit does
  not replace, modify, or re-weight the preregistered primary analysis.
