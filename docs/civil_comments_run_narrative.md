## Interpretation and my read (for your review — manuscript NOT modified)

- **21 of 24 cells survived Holm.** These are the identity-mention attributes for which an evaluator's rating retains residual conditional dependence on identity among comments with identical human annotation vectors.
- The signal is **shared across architectures** for: LGBTQ, black, female, male, muslim, other_religions, white (flagged by ≥2 evaluators). Shared rejections are the more interesting story — they suggest the residual dependence is not a quirk of one model family.
- **Calibration check:** the negative controls should be near-null (≈1–2 of 24 significant by chance); if they are, the permutation machinery is behaving. The binned-Z sensitivity should broadly agree with the exact-Z primary; large disagreement would mean the result depends on how Z is conditioned and should be reported as such.
- **What this does NOT show:** a rejection means only residual conditional statistical dependence on an identity-mention attribute given the observed human labels. It is not evidence of discrimination, unfairness, causal use of identity, or that identity language is irrelevant to toxicity, and it does not treat human annotations as ground truth.
- **Confidence / caveats:** this is a secondary replication family, not confirmatory; the Gemini branch carried more technical exclusions than GPT/Claude (quota floods + the 11 Wi-Fi network failures), so weight the Gemini missingness audit when interpreting its cells. The exclusions are A-blind by construction, but the audit is the place to confirm no per-identity association slipped in.
